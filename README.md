# 3-D coincidence momentum imaging and 400 nm driven HHG

A 400 nm driven high harmonic generation (HHG) light source is used to perform 3-D coincidence momentum imaging in gas-phase atomic and molecular systems.
Vacuum ultraviolet two-photon ionization and dissociation dynamics are studied across varioius systems using 9.3 eV (133 nm) femtosecond pulses.
The first experiments were conducted on two-photon ionization dynamics in Ar.
This was followed by experiments in molecular oxygen where both resonant two-photon ionization and ion-pair formation were studied.
Non-resonant two-photon ionization experiments were also conducted in molecular nitrogen.
